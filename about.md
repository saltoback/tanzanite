# Tanzanite

Purple mod menu for Geometry Dash. **Press the key above Tab (`²` on AZERTY, `` ` `` on QWERTY) to open or close it.**

- **Player**: noclip, speedhack (adjustable speed)
- **Level**: safe mode, auto safe mode, accurate percentage
- **Labels**: cheat indicator, FPS, CPS, noclip accuracy and deaths, attempts, jumps, level time, clock, best run, custom message; adjustable corner, size and opacity
- **Macro mode**: one button, then a full interface in the level (record / play, pause, frame by frame, speed, hitboxes, trajectory, checkpoints, save)
